# Motif library by sector

Subject ideas for new plates (v1: one electric ink; sector pigments are retired, ignore any pigment notes below).

Prompt fragments to drop into the `[SUBJECT]` slot of the formula in `SKILL.md`. Each sector
has a **lead motif** for the hero, plus alternates. Choose motifs that show what the sector's
data measures.

## Housing & Land
- **Lead:** Victorian terrace in flat elevation, chimney stacks in rhythm, bay windows, engraved hatching.
- Council tower block in elevation beside semi-detached 1930s houses (tenure mix).
- Tithe-map or Land Registry style plot outlines, with field boundaries as fine double lines.
- Scaffolding and a crane over a half-built close (completions and starts).
- Keys on a ring, a front door with a brass number (affordability and ownership).
- Pigment: London stock brick `#b07c2c`.

## Energy & Industry
- **Lead:** steel lattice pylons crossing hatched farmland toward cooling towers.
- Offshore wind turbines on an engraved North Sea swell; Admiralty soundings in the water.
- Gasholder frames (Kings Cross, Oval, Bromley-by-Bow type) as pure ironwork line.
- Coal pithead winding gear (historic generation mix, History tie-in).
- Electricity or gas meter dial faces; National Grid substation plan drawing.
- A factory sawtooth roof in elevation (the ONS manufacturing series).

## Economy & Public Finance
- **Lead:** guilloché rosette and banknote-style border around an *empty* cartouche.
- Coin-edge milling as a horizontal band; stacked coins in engraved profile (no monarch).
- Treasury ledger page with ruled columns and double underlines (totals).
- Red Budget despatch box, closed, engraved (the signal colour is allowed here).
- Bank of England Threadneedle Street columns as an architectural elevation (generic, no logo).
- Shipping containers on a quayside crane (trade balance).

## People & Society
- **Lead:** census enumeration form, ruled and blank, with a pen nib resting on it.
- Rows of identical windows in a tenement or terrace, each slightly different inside (population).
- NHS-style hospital corridor in perspective, line-only, with no logos.
- School desk with an inkwell; a library card catalogue drawer.
- Avoid realistic faces. People appear as distant silhouettes in crowds, or not at all.

## Government & Politics
- **Lead:** House of Commons seating plan drawn as an engraved diagram from above.
- Hansard page columns as texture: ruled, justified and illegible.
- Ballot box with a wax seal; a stack of polling cards; a count-hall table.
- Act of Parliament title page frame (blank), with fine rules and ornament corners.
- Do not use the Royal Arms, a crown or the portcullis logo. All are protected.

## Infrastructure
- **Lead:** Victorian railway viaduct crossing a valley, engraved with hatched arches.
- Motorway interchange shown as a plan drawing (the Spaghetti Junction pattern) in contour-style lines.
- Canal lock gates; the Forth Bridge cantilever silhouette; Tyne bridges.
- Railway timetable grid and Bradshaw-style page ruling as texture.
- Fibre and water pipe cross-sections drawn as survey diagrams.

## Regions & Places
- **Lead:** Ordnance Survey contour fragment with kilometre grid squares and spot heights.
- Admiralty chart coastline with soundings, compass rose and rhumb lines.
- Tile-map hexes or squares of local authorities (echoes the `TileMap` component), engraved.
- Booth-style street map, with streets shaded by hatch density instead of colour.
- One-nation-each landscape set: Pennines, Highlands lochs, Welsh valleys terrace, Antrim coast.

## History & Change
- **Lead:** archive folder with a typed label strip and string tie, sepia second plate.
- A series of the same street shown as engraved strips at different dates.
- Ration book, ration coupon perforations, a 1940s identity card frame (blank).
- Pre-decimal coin outlines (shilling, penny) with no monarch portrait.
- Steam-era mill chimney alongside a modern data centre box.

## Cross-sector instruments (any sector, small vignettes)
Theodolite, brass sextant, tide gauge, Kew-pattern barometer, chronometer, the Greenwich
meridian brass strip, imperial standard yard plaque, surveyor's chain, slide rule.
