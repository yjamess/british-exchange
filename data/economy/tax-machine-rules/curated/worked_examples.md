# Worked examples (human-readable)

Educational illustration of dated statutory income-tax and employee Class 1 NICs rules — not tax advice and not a substitute for HMRC guidance or a personal tax calculation. Hypothetical taxable employment income only; Scotland uses a labelled alternate band table. Statutory incidence (who the law charges) is not the same as economic incidence (who ultimately bears the cost). Modelled liabilities are not observed population tax paid.

Exclusions: benefits in kind; pension contributions / relief; marriage allowance; blind person's allowance; student loan repayments; savings / dividend income (starting rate for savings and dividend rates out of scope); personal allowance taper for income above £100,000 (examples stay at or below £100,000); employer Class 1 NICs (employer stage not modelled); married women's reduced NICs rates / other category letters (category A only); corporation tax, VAT, CGT, council tax.

Tax year for examples: **2025–26** (E/W/NI income-tax + employee NICs rates for these incomes match 2026–27 in this pin).
