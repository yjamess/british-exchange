# National DNO topology — Blocked

**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Domain:** 07 Power Networks · Gate 3 gated item

## Why Blocked

There is **no single official national distribution-network topology** (all substations, feeders, spare capacity by node) published under one open licence for Great Britain.

- **14 DNO licence areas** publish LTDS / open data on different schedules, schemas and licences.  
- **Local spare capacity by node** is not a national Official Statistics series.  
- Inventing a UK circuit map from incomplete operator feeds would fail Observatory honesty rules.

## What would unblock it

Any one of:

1. A **partner-supplied**, licence-cleared national topology product (NESO/TO/DNO consortium) with CRS + update cadence, **or**  
2. Explicit product decision to ship **one named DNO area only** (pilot) with that operator’s open data URL + licence pinned, **or**  
3. Keep Study C **Border anatomy** as the Networks flagship interim and leave Invisible Grid deferred.

## Related that *can* proceed without DNO GIS

- NESO **TEC Register** (connections queue) — open CSV  
- Ready **ET 5.6** interconnector flows (already live)  
- NESO thermal constraint costs (later; boundary mapping required)

**No action needed from the product owner unless** you want option 1 or 2 above.
